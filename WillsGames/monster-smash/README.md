## How to Play

### Objective
Drive your monster truck around the arena and smash into junker cars to score points. The more cars you smash, the higher your score!

### Controls

**Keyboard:**
- `W` / `↑` - Move up
- `S` / `↓` - Move down
- `A` / `←` - Move left
- `D` / `→` - Move right
- `R` - Restart show

**Touch Controls:**
- Use the on-screen directional pad to drive
- Tap the SMASH button for boost
- Perfect for tablets

### Scoring
- Smash junker cars to score points
- Track your total smashes in the sidebar
- Try to beat your high score!

## Development Story

Will's specific request was "a monster truck that smashes things." We kept it simple: a truck that moves around, cars to smash, and a score counter. No complex game-over conditions, no time limits - just pure smashing fun.

The SMASH button on the touch controls was Will's idea. Even though it doesn't do anything different from driving into cars, it feels great to tap before a big hit!

## Pit Crew Tips

1. **Line up your shot** - Aim carefully before you smash
2. **Keep moving** - Don't get stuck in corners
3. **Chase the junkers** - They appear across the arena
4. **Use momentum** - Speed makes for bigger smashes
