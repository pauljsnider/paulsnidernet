Stitch Racing brings intergalactic competition to Will's Arcade. Race as Stitch or the Red Rival on an infinite scrolling track. Play solo or challenge a friend in 2-player mode!

## How to Play

### Game Modes
- **1 Player:** Race against time and obstacles
- **2 Players:** Side-by-side racing with split controls

### Character Selection
- **Stitch** - The blue speedster
- **Red Rival** - The crimson challenger

### Controls

**Player 1 (Arrows):**
- `↑` - Accelerate
- `↓` - Brake/Reverse
- `←` - Steer left
- `→` - Steer right

**Player 2 (WASD):**
- `W` - Accelerate
- `S` - Brake/Reverse
- `A` - Steer left
- `D` - Steer right

**Mobile Controls:**
- Toggle on-screen D-pad for touch devices
- Perfect for racing on tablets

### Objective
- Complete laps faster than your opponent
- Navigate the scrolling track
- Avoid obstacles and stay on course
- First to cross the finish line wins!

## Development Story

Stitch Racing was the most ambitious game in Will's Arcade. It needed:
- Character selection
- Multiple game modes
- 2-player input handling
- Custom graphics
- A complete menu system

We built it in phases, adding each feature incrementally during our coding sessions. The result is a fully-featured racing game that works great on desktop and mobile.

## Racing Tips

### For Beginners
1. **Learn the track** - Watch the scrolling pattern
2. **Smooth inputs** - Don't jerk the wheel
3. **Practice solo** - Master controls in 1P mode first

### For Competitors
1. **Inside lines** - Shortest path wins races
2. **Anticipate turns** - Look ahead on the track
3. **Challenge friends** - 2P mode is where it gets intense
