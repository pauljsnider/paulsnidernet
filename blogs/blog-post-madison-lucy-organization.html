Madison & Lucy Make a Website in an Afternoon

Paul Snider Technology Executive & MBA

Madison and her friend Lucy came home from school with an idea: they wanted to help people organize their rooms. They'd just finished reorganizing Madison's room and thought it was pretty fun. Plus, they took before-and-after photos that actually looked good.

"We should make a website," Madison said. "We can call it Madison & Lucy Organization Service."

Sure, why not.

The Concept

The pitch was simple: help people organize closets, cabinets, and rooms. Make spaces less chaotic. Their tagline? "Organize Your Life, Create Your Getaway."

Madison explained: "Like, if you need a place to escape your brothers."

Fair point.

Designing in Canva

The girls opened Canva and picked fonts. They made a logo. Lucy chose a color scheme. Madison sketched out what pages they needed: home, about, services, gallery, contact.

"We want it to look nice but not too fancy," Madison said.

They spent maybe 30 minutes in Canva getting it to look how they wanted.

Building It with Antigravity

We fired up Google's Antigravity IDE and the girls described what they wanted. Antigravity turned their Canva designs into working HTML and CSS while we sat at the kitchen table.

They added their before-and-after photos from Madison's room. The "before" showed typical kid chaos. The "after" showed everything organized and in its place.

Total time from Canva to live website? About an afternoon.

The Tech

Simple setup:

  • Canva for design
  • Antigravity to turn it into code
  • GitHub Pages for hosting
  • GoDaddy domain name

They tested it on their phones, made a few tweaks, and we pushed it live at madisonsnider.com. Done.

The Fun Part

What made this project work was keeping it simple. The girls had an idea, spent an afternoon building it, and now they have a real website. They learned that Canva + Antigravity can turn a concept into a live site pretty quickly.

Will they organize more rooms? Maybe. Are they serious about scaling their business empire? Not really. Did they have fun making a website? Definitely.

Check it out at madisonsnider.com.
